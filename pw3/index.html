Dogs and colors

Dogs see fewer colors than people. They mostly distinguish blue and yellow, while red looks to them like a dark gray.

Smart friends

An average dog can learn about 165 words and gestures. The smartest dogs can understand more than 250.

Playtime

Dogs play not only for fun. Games help them train, stay healthy and build trust with their owners.
